# NeurIPS 2026 Submission Readiness Checklist

## Formatting compliance
- [ ] Paper uses `neurips_2026.sty` with `default` option.
- [ ] Main body is within 9 content pages (figures and tables included).
- [ ] Single PDF contains: paper → optional appendices → mandatory paper checklist.
- [ ] PDF file size is under 50 MB.
- [ ] Supplementary ZIP (if any) is under 100 MB.
- [ ] No margin, font size, or spacing modifications from the style file defaults.

## Anonymization
- [ ] Author names, affiliations, and acknowledgments removed from paper.
- [ ] Self-citations written in third person ("Smith et al. [1]" not "our previous work [1]").
- [ ] No links to non-anonymized repositories, personal pages, or identifiable resources.
- [ ] Supplementary code stripped of author metadata, personal paths, and identifying comments.
- [ ] Figures and tables do not contain identifying watermarks or logos.

## Mandatory paper checklist
- [ ] NeurIPS paper checklist is present in the PDF after any technical appendices.
- [ ] Every item is answered Yes, No, or NA with justification where appropriate.
- [ ] Every "Yes" answer has supporting content in the manuscript.
- [ ] Every "No" answer has a clear, honest justification.

## Required and encouraged content
- [ ] Separate Limitations section is present with specific, actionable content.
- [ ] Broader societal impacts are discussed somewhere in the paper.
- [ ] Ethics review guidelines have been read and the paper conforms to them.

## Scientific quality
- [ ] Main claims are explicit, scoped, and match the evidence presented.
- [ ] Every headline claim maps to a figure, table, theorem, or appendix anchor.
- [ ] Strongest relevant baselines are covered and fairly compared.
- [ ] For empirical papers: error bars or confidence intervals are reported.
- [ ] For empirical papers: multi-seed or multi-run results are included.
- [ ] For empirical papers: all training details are specified (optimizer, LR, batch size, epochs, etc.).
- [ ] For theoretical papers: full assumptions stated and complete proofs included.
- [ ] Ablation studies are present for multi-component methods.

## Reproducibility
- [ ] Code, data, and/or instructions for reproduction are provided (supplementary or anonymized URL).
- [ ] Exact commands and environment specifications are included.
- [ ] Compute resources (GPU hours, hardware type) are reported.
- [ ] Data availability statement is present if the paper uses datasets.
- [ ] Dataset licenses and creator citations are included.

## Dual submission compliance
- [ ] No overlapping archival submission exists by any overlapping set of authors.
- [ ] Any concurrent non-archival workshop submissions are identified and permissible.

## Administrative
- [ ] All authors have OpenReview profiles.
- [ ] Abstract was submitted by May 4, 2026 AOE.
- [ ] Full paper and supplementary submitted by May 6, 2026 AOE.

## Writing quality
- [ ] Abstract states problem, method, evidence, and takeaway in scoped language.
- [ ] Introduction states gap and contributions clearly.
- [ ] Related work differentiates against closest prior work, not just surveys it.
- [ ] Results section interprets numbers, not just lists them.
- [ ] Conclusion does not overclaim beyond the evidence.
- [ ] Figures and tables have self-contained captions.

## AC meta-review readiness (new for 2026)
- [ ] Top 3–5 likely reviewer concerns are pre-identified and addressed in the paper.
- [ ] Paper structure makes it easy for an AC to summarize the contribution in one paragraph.
- [ ] Key strengths are clearly articulated (not buried or implicit).
- [ ] Weaknesses are acknowledged honestly in the Limitations section rather than left for reviewers to discover.

## Final pass
- [ ] Paper has been read end-to-end for coherence and narrative flow.
- [ ] All cross-references, figure numbers, and table numbers are correct.
- [ ] Bibliography is complete and consistent in format.
- [ ] No placeholder text, TODO markers, or draft comments remain.
